A Journey of Growth: 15 Days with Amroha Police

This internship was far more than a technical training program—it was a transformative 15-day law enforcement fellowship that pushed me out of my comfort zone. Arriving in Amroha, I was initially an outsider in an unfamiliar environment. However, this challenge turned into an extraordinary opportunity for personal and professional growth.

Over the course of the fellowship, I had the privilege of interacting with more than 45+ incredible people—including senior state cyber cell officers, DSP Anjali Kataria Ma'am, national security advisors like Rakshit Tandon & Amit Dubey, coordinating student leaders, and brilliant co-interns. Engaging in deep technical case discussions broadened my horizons significantly.

From late-night forensic debugging simulations to analyzing complex UPI transaction trails, every single day taught me something new. I leave this program not just with technical mastery over mobile forensics, OSINT tools, and SOC platforms, but with invaluable life lessons, operational discipline, and a strong network of professional mentors across law enforcement.

Rohit Pal
Cyber Security Fellow · Full-Stack Web Developer
